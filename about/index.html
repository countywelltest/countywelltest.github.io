About County Well Test

County Well Test is an independent reference to what private well water testing costs and how to get it done, county by county.

Labs and health departments publish their prices and rules in many different places and formats. We gather them into one page per county, keep the original wording where it matters, and link every line back to its source so you can confirm it yourself.

The site is written and maintained under the pen name Dana Whitcomb. We are not a laboratory, not a health department and not affiliated with any agency or lab listed. We do not test water, interpret results or give health advice.

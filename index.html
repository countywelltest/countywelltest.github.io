Why a well needs its own test

City water is tested by the utility. A private well is tested by you. Wisconsin's health department suggests bacteria and nitrate every year and arsenic and lead every five years, and the county pages show where you can get each test done and what it costs.

Many labs mail a kit or let you pick one up, and some counties run free programs. We list the rules each one posts, such as how fast a sample must get back.

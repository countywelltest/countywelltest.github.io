How we verify

Every fact on County Well Test comes from a primary source: the lab's own page, or the county, state or federal agency page that publishes it.

The rules we follow

  • Primary sources only. Prices, turnaround times, hours, kit and drop-off rules, certification statements and free-program eligibility are copied from the lab's or agency's own page. Each appears with a link to that page.
  • Dated. Each fact carries the date we last read it ("Last verified"). Pages are rechecked at least monthly; anything older than 30 days is flagged for re-verification.
  • No estimates, no invention. If a source does not state something, we write "not posted" rather than guess.
  • Verbatim guidance. Health and testing guidance is quoted word-for-word from the agency that published it and attributed. We add no health interpretation of our own.
  • Conflicts. When two source pages disagree (for example a county web page and an older printed brochure), we use the most recently dated web page and say so.
  • Not affiliated. We are an independent guide, not a laboratory, health department or government agency, and are not affiliated with any of them.

Corrections

Labs and agencies: if a price or rule here differs from your current page, the current page is correct and we will update ours at the next check. A dedicated corrections address will be listed here.

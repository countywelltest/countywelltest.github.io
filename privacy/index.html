Privacy

What {BRAND} collects, and what it does not. There are no cookies and no advertising trackers on this site.

Visitor statistics

County Well Test uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless measurement tool run by Cloudflare. It is on for this site. It counts pageviews and visits, and it records aggregate figures such as which pages were read, referring sites, browser and device type, and country. It does not use cookies, does not collect personal information such as your name or email, does not follow you across other websites, and is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

This site does not set cookies, and it does not use advertising trackers, social-media pixels, or fingerprinting for ads.

Email signups

If you use the email form, you are asking to join the well-test-reminder list for County Well Test (A reminder when testing season starts). We store only:

  • Your email address
  • The site and list you joined
  • The date and time of the signup
  • The version of the consent text shown on the form
  • A confirmation status (pending or confirmed)

Signups are stored in a database operated by us on Cloudflare (a Worker at https://lists.noticelist.workers.dev and a D1 database named mogul-signups). Each site has its own list. We do not store your IP address or browser user-agent with the signup. A short-lived, hashed key may be used only to slow down spam, and it expires automatically.

The form includes a hidden field to catch automated spam. A submission that fills in that field is ignored.

We never share or sell your email.

We do not send marketing email, and we have not sent any email to this list. Addresses are stored as “pending confirmation” until a confirmation-email step is switched on. When that happens, we will send a confirmation link first, and only write for the purpose you signed up for. Every future email will include a way to unsubscribe. We will not send list email without an explicit go-ahead from the publisher.

What we do not collect

  • No cookies
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking
  • No sale or sharing of email addresses
  • No payment details (this site does not take payments)

How long we keep a signup

We keep an address on the list until you ask to be removed, or until the list is retired. To be removed, use the unsubscribe link in a future email, or use the corrections path on the About or methodology page to ask us to delete it.

This privacy page describes the site as of Nov 19, 2026. If collection practices change, we will update this page first.

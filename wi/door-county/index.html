What to know before you test

Guidance as published

  • The Department of Natural Resources (DNR) recommends that well water is tested at least once a year for bacteria and nitrates, or anytime that you notice a change in how your water looks, tastes, or smells.
    Source: co.door.wi.gov
  • Private well users should test their private well for: Bacteria and nitrate at least once a year.
    Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov
  • Arsenic and lead at least once every five years.
    Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov
  • When testing, it is best to use a Wisconsin certified lab.
    Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov

Wisconsin context

  • About 30 percent of Wisconsin residents get their drinking water from private wells.
    Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov
  • Private well owners are responsible for delivering safe drinking water to their households.
    Source: epa.gov

FAQ

How much does a well water bacteria test cost in Door County?

Posted bacteria (coliform / E. coli) test prices for labs on this page: UW-Oshkosh ERIC Lab $25; Analytichem LLC $30; Ephraim Well Water Testing $30; Baileys Harbor Wastewater Treatment Plant $30; Wisconsin State Laboratory of Hygiene (WSLH) $35.00. Source: co.door.wi.gov

Is free well testing available in Door County?

Free well testing through WellTAP if all apply: the well serves someone who is pregnant, a child 12 or under, or a person with a weakened immune system; it serves your primary residence; a financial or other burden prevents you from testing on your own. Source: co.door.wi.gov

How often should a Wisconsin private well be tested?

The Wisconsin Department of Health Services says: bacteria and nitrate at least once a year; arsenic and lead at least once every five years. Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov

Is there a test package for an FHA or VA home loan?

The Wisconsin State Laboratory of Hygiene lists a HUD/FHA-VA Loan Package at $146.00 (7 business days; RUSH $292.00, 2 business days). Check your lender's own requirements. Source: slh.wisc.edu

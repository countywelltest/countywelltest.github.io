What to know before you test

Guidance, as published

  • Oneida County Health Department advises all private well owners to have their well water tested for coliform bacteria and nitrates annually, especially if infants under six months of age or women of childbearing age drinking the water, or sooner if changes in taste, odor or appearance are noticed, or there is an unexplained illness.
    Source: publichealth.oneidacountywi.gov
  • Private well users should test their private well for: Bacteria and nitrate at least once a year.
    Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov
  • Arsenic and lead at least once every five years.
    Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov
  • When testing, it is best to use a Wisconsin certified lab.
    Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov

Sample and drop-off rules

  • Oneida County Health Department Water Analysis Lab: Sample bottles, instructions and the Request for Water Analysis form from the Health Department, 100 W. Keenan St, Rhinelander Source: publichealth.oneidacountywi.gov
  • Oneida County Health Department Water Analysis Lab: Submit the sample within 24 hours of collection, with the form and the testing fee Source: publichealth.oneidacountywi.gov
  • Oneida County Health Department Water Analysis Lab: Monday through Wednesday 8:30 am - 3:30 pm (schedule subject to change during holidays) Source: publichealth.oneidacountywi.gov
  • Wisconsin State Laboratory of Hygiene (WSLH): Kit shipment to you is free; you pay return shipment to the lab Source: slh.wisc.edu
  • Wisconsin State Laboratory of Hygiene (WSLH): Do not send payment with samples; the lab bills you after testing Source: slh.wisc.edu
  • Wisconsin State Laboratory of Hygiene (WSLH): Do not ship samples on Fridays for Saturday delivery Source: slh.wisc.edu
  • UW-Stevens Point Water & Environmental Analysis Lab (WEAL): Bottles are mailed at no cost, or picked up at the lab Source: uwsp.edu
  • UW-Stevens Point Water & Environmental Analysis Lab (WEAL): Paid at sample acceptance: cash, check or credit card; include a check payable to WEAL if shipping Source: uwsp.edu

Wisconsin context

  • About 30 percent of Wisconsin residents get their drinking water from private wells.
    Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov
  • Private well owners are responsible for delivering safe drinking water to their households.
    Source: epa.gov

Common questions

How much does a well water bacteria test cost in Oneida County?

Posted bacteria (coliform / E. coli) test prices for labs on this page: Oneida County Health Department Water Analysis Lab $27.00 (from a list dated Jan 14, 2025; call to confirm before you go); Wisconsin State Laboratory of Hygiene (WSLH) $35.00; Oneida County Health Department Water Analysis Lab $52.00 (from a list dated Jan 14, 2025; call to confirm before you go); Oneida County Health Department Water Analysis Lab $82.00 (from a list dated Jan 14, 2025; call to confirm before you go). Source: publichealth.oneidacountywi.gov

Is free well testing available in Oneida County?

Families with pregnant women or infants may qualify for free testing; call the Oneida County WIC office at (715) 369-6109. Source: publichealth.oneidacountywi.gov

How often should a Wisconsin private well be tested?

The Wisconsin Department of Health Services says: bacteria and nitrate at least once a year; arsenic and lead at least once every five years. Source: dhs.wisconsin.gov

Is there a test package for an FHA or VA home loan?

The Wisconsin State Laboratory of Hygiene lists a HUD/FHA-VA Loan Package at $146.00 (7 business days; RUSH $292.00, 2 business days). Check your lender's own requirements. Source: slh.wisc.edu

When can I drop off a well water sample in Oneida County?

Oneida County Health Department Water Analysis Lab: Monday through Wednesday 8:30 am - 3:30 pm (schedule subject to change during holidays). Source: publichealth.oneidacountywi.gov

Where do I get a well water test kit in Oneida County?
